DIGITAL ORDERING: PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS

Learn how to handle common problems when using digital ordering services. Click each problem to view its suggested solution.

1. Payment and Transaction Problems

Payment was declined.

Check your available balance and payment details. Try another accepted payment method if necessary.

QR payment is not scanning.

Adjust your phone's brightness and position it properly in front of the scanner.

Card payment is unsuccessful.

Check whether the card is valid and has sufficient funds. Ask staff to check the payment terminal if the issue continues.

Payment is still pending.

Check the transaction status in your payment application. Do not repeat the payment until its status is confirmed.

2. System Performance Problems

Kiosk screen is lagging.

Wait for the current selection to respond before tapping again.

Touchscreen is not responding.

Avoid repeatedly pressing the screen. Ask restaurant staff to check the kiosk.

Mobile application is running slowly.

Check your internet connection and close unnecessary applications before reopening the ordering app.

Application suddenly crashes.

Reopen the application and check your order history before placing another order.

Menu is not loading.

Check your internet connection if using a mobile app. For kiosk problems, ask staff for assistance.

3. Order Accuracy Problems

Wrong food item is selected.

Return to the menu and select the correct item before submitting the order.

Ordered item becomes unavailable.

Ask restaurant staff about available alternatives before proceeding.

Duplicate items appear in the cart.

Remove the extra item before confirming your purchase.

Received food does not match the confirmed order.

Present your receipt or order confirmation to restaurant staff and report the incorrect item.

Wrong meal size is displayed.

Review the selected size and correct it before submitting the order.

4. Order Status and Update Problems

No order confirmation appears.

Check your order history or receipt, if available. Ask staff to verify whether the order was received.

Order is taking longer than expected.

Present your order number to restaurant staff and ask about its status.

No order notification is received.

Check the application directly for updates, if the feature is available.

Order is marked completed but has not been received.

Show your order number or receipt to staff for verification.

Incorrect order status is displayed.

Ask restaurant staff to confirm the actual status of your order.

5. Mobile Application Problems

Unable to log in.

Check your username and password. Use the application's password recovery option if needed.

Application will not open.

Check your internet connection and update the application if an update is available.

Cart suddenly becomes empty.

Check your order history before rebuilding or submitting the order.

Application displays outdated menu information.

Refresh the menu or contact the restaurant to confirm current availability and prices.

Application keeps disconnecting.

Switch to a more stable internet connection and check whether your order was submitted before trying again.
